# NXTGEN Reels: Posting Kit

Five 9:16 reels (1080×1920, ~28–37s) for Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Facebook Reels, targeted at Nottingham & Nottinghamshire.

> **Before posting anything:** the phone number, review count (214), rating (4.9), "1,500+ installs" and all three customer reviews in Reel 4 are **placeholders** from the website prototype (`src/config/site.ts`, `src/content/index.ts`). Replace them with real figures and genuine reviews (with the customers' permission), then re-run `npm run voice && npm run render`. UK consumer law (DMCC Act 2024) and the ASA treat fake or unverifiable reviews/claims in ads as unlawful.

## How each reel is built to convert

| Hook technique | Why it works |
| --- | --- |
| Hook text + illustration fully on screen at **frame 0** | The first frame is the thumbnail and decides the scroll-stop |
| "Nottingham" said or shown in the first 2 seconds | Local relevance; the viewer feels it's *for them* |
| Cut every 2–4s, white flash, punch-in zoom, constant motion | Keeps retention above the drop-off curve |
| Big kinetic captions | Most reels are watched muted; captions are also read by the algorithm |
| Story-style progress bar | Signals "short", nudges watch-to-end |
| Logo on every frame + end card with URL, phone, CTA button | Brand recall + clear next step |
| One CTA only: **instant online estimate** | Lowest-friction action → highest conversion |

## Posting & SEO checklist

- **Location tag:** "Nottingham, United Kingdom" on every post (or the specific town for Reel 4/5 variants).
- **Keyword first:** the first line of each caption contains the search phrase. Public Instagram posts are indexed by Google, and TikTok/YouTube search reads the caption and on-screen text.
- **Cover/thumbnail:** use frame 0 (already designed as the cover).
- **Audio:** voiceover (local Nottingham accent), background music and sound effects are all baked in and royalty-free. When posting, keep the original audio (don't add a second track over the voice).
- **Link in bio:** point it straight at `/get-a-quote` with UTM tags, e.g. `?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=reel&utm_campaign=reel1-winter`, one per reel, so you can see which hook converts.
- **Schedule:** 1 reel every 2–3 days. UK engagement peaks around 7–9pm weekdays and 9–11am Saturday. Order: 1 → 2 → 3 → 5 → 4 (after real reviews are in).
- **Paid boost:** put £5–10/day behind the best organic performer after 48h, targeting a 15-mile radius of Nottingham, homeowners aged 28–65.
- **Google Business Profile:** upload each MP4 there as well; it helps the local "air conditioning near me" pack.
- **A/B voices:** Reels 1, 3, 5 use the *Puck* voice and 2, 4 use *Zephyr*. Swap `voice` in `src/reels.ts` to test.
