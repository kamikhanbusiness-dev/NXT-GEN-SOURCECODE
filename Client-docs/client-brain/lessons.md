# Lessons (how to make better calls with this client)

Add a line whenever something teaches you how this client works. Newest at the bottom.

- Ian writes short instruction lists without context ("put the minimum prices to £1600"). Don't apply them literally across the whole site; confirm the scope of each instruction before it touches pricing.
- Ian pushes for fast dates ("next Friday"). He accepts a realistic date when it's explained honestly and tied to his content. Always link dates to content arriving.
- His main worry is return on investment ("need to see good sales"). Frame features and replies around enquiries, calls and quote requests.
- He has lots of material on Instagram. Photos may come as Instagram posts/screenshots; ask for originals for quality.
- He said "you can keep it active?" — he expects a done-for-you service. Be clear what the £30/month covers so small requests don't creep beyond 1 hour/month.
